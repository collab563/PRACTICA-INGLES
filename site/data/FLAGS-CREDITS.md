## Country flag images

The bundled flag SVGs and country-name metadata are derived from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) by Panayiotis Lipiridis and are distributed under the MIT License. The license text is included in `Flag-Icons-LICENSE.txt`. The SVGs are stored with the site in `images/flags/`, so the practice does not rely on a third-party image host.
