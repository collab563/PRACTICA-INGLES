## English IPA pronunciation data

The British English IPA word list in `phonetics.js` is derived from Britfone 3.0.1 by Jose Llarena. It provides Received Pronunciation / Standard Southern British transcriptions and is distributed under the MIT License. The original data file and its license are included here as `britfone.main.3.0.1.csv` and `Britfone-LICENSE.txt`.

Source: https://github.com/JoseLlarena/Britfone
